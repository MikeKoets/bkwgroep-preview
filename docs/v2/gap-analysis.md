# V2 gap analysis — original and V1

Browser captures: five original routes and six V1 routes, at 1440 and 390 px. Both sites were actually rendered in Chrome. Original initial captures include its cookie consent; additional clean captures reject optional cookies.

| Area | Original | V1 preview | V2 response |
|---|---|---|---|
| First impression | Authentic team; dominant Hornbach branding, heavy dark background | Same darkened panoramic photo, generic large headline | Clear split proposition and visible craft photograph |
| Brand | Orange/charcoal recognizable | Brand retained, otherwise generic rounded template | Consistent orange linework, restrained frames, deliberate typography |
| Services | Real pictures give context | Four equal icon cards | Four photographic service entries with distinct next steps |
| Photography | Useful originals, some edited diagonals and partner signage | Dark overlays further obscure detail; nonresponsive files | New selection and source-resolution crops; no text overlay on photos |
| Conversion | Emergency calling prominent, many repeated pitches | One CTA hierarchy for every service | Urgent phone path separate from planned/personal/business work |
| Mobile | Heavy consent/navigation | Long one-column stack, phone buried, huge dark hero | Compact header with call control, focused drawer, readable split hero |
| Routing | Server-rendered page changes reset naturally | SPA retains previous scroll, no restoration component | Regression-tested history-key restoration with anchors |
| Credibility | Strong claims require reconfirmation | Cautious facts, but business photo alt implies a client relation | Verified service/contact facts; no client/certification claims |
| Consistency | Dated but cohesive | Repeated cards and CTA wrappers | Shared tokens with distinct service-led content |

V1 is lighter than the original, but this alone does not demonstrate a premium design. Source contact details and genuine photography remain strengths to preserve. No new stock or generated project images are needed if original unedited photography is sufficient.
